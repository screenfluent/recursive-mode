# Chart a map

1. Use `recursive-grilling` and `recursive-domain-modeling` as needed to name the **destination**. Obtain human approval of the destination before a durable write.
2. Explore breadth-first: identify sharp questions, dependencies, and surrounding fog. If no meaningful fog remains and the work fits one focused session, stop and ask how the human wants to proceed; create no map.
3. Create `MAP.md` with the Destination and Notes filled in, including the domain, skills every session should consult, and standing preferences for the effort. Create the units that are sharp now and keep later questions under `Not yet specified`.
4. Create all unit files before adding blocker links, then validate links and cycles in a second pass.
5. Ask the human to approve the charted map. Resolve no unit in the chart invocation.

Complete when the human-approved destination, initial units, blocker graph, fog, and out-of-scope boundary are durable and `validate` passes.
